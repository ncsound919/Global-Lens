# Context: Economic State of Black America
- Wealth Gap: The median wealth of white families is historically significantly higher than that of Black families. Focus actions on financial literacy, small business ownership, and real estate acquisition.
- Housing: Homeownership rates hover around 43-45% for Black Americans compared to over 70% for white Americans. Redlining and credit algorithms continue to represent historical pain points.
- Local Mobility: Focus heavily on the "Black Dollar Velocity"—how long money stays within community-owned businesses—and structural employment shifts in Tech and Green Energy.
