# Domestic Equity & Hyper-Local Impact Lens

When analyzing events through this lens, focus strictly on the hyper-local community impact, especially for marginalized and historically underinvested neighborhoods. Analyze how macroeconomic trends, political shifts, or global occurrences directly affect:

1. Local housing markets, gentrification, and tenant rights.
2. Domestic wealth building, small business ownership, and local economic resilience.
3. Municipal and state-level policy consequences on daily community life.
4. Access to local healthcare, public education, and essential neighborhood services.

Keep the focus narrow, specific, and directly applicable to the immediate, local community scale, addressing systemic inequities within domestic borders.
